Requirements

- Basic statistics and machine learning concepts
- Visualisation with Tableau, Power BI or Python
- Curiosity, attention to detail and teamwork

Eligibility and other information
12-month internship starting June 2027. You must be enrolled on a degree and return to it after the internship.
